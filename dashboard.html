About Sectional Tests
Each sectional is a timed 21-question practice test designed to simulate real GMAT conditions. The goal is not just accuracy — it's building time management under pressure. Questions use a streak method: answer correctly and the difficulty increases, struggle and it adjusts down — exactly how the real GMAT adapts to you. Aim to complete all 21 questions within the 45-minute window every time.
