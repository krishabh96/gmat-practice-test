Export & Deploy
Download your question bank and deploy to Vercel
📥 Export questions.js
Download an updated questions.js file with all your questions baked in. Replace the existing one in your project folder, then redeploy to Vercel. Your users will instantly see the new questions — no setup required.
📦 Export questions.json (backup)
Download a JSON backup of all questions. Use this to restore your question bank on a new machine or share with a co-admin using the Import button in Questions tab.
🚀 Deploy to Vercel — 3 Steps
Step 1: Click "Download questions.js" above
Step 2: Replace the questions.js file in your project folder
Step 3: Push to GitHub — Vercel auto-deploys in ~30 seconds

Your friends visit your Vercel URL and get the updated questions immediately.
