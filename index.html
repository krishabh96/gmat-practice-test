Our Story

Why We Built This

I believe quality education should be accessible to everyone. Preparing for the GMAT can be expensive, and for many students, even practice materials and mock tests can feel out of reach. Not everyone can afford to spend $100 or more just for practice.

That is exactly why I built this platform — to offer affordable sectional and full-length mock tests for GMAT aspirants. The aim is simple: give students access to practice that feels close to the official GMAT style, without the heavy price tag.

This platform is growing step by step. Every test you take and every bit of feedback you share helps improve the page further. Your support will help expand the question bank and improve the quality of the tests.

If you are preparing for the GMAT, I hope this page becomes a useful, affordable, and reliable part of your journey. Practice for free, grow with the platform.
